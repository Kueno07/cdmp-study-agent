# Data Governance — Ch.3 (11%)
| Concept | Testable angle | Common confusions |
|---|---|---|
| DG vs Data Management | Authority/decision rights vs execution | Treating as synonyms; reversing strategic vs operational |
| Operating models | Centralized / Decentralized / Federated | Centralized for diverse orgs; federated ≠ replicated |
| DG bodies | Council, Steward, Owner, Office roles | Council defining physical models; Owner (accountability) vs Steward (responsibility) |
| Policy/Standard/Procedure | General→specific hierarchy | Interchangeable; wrong order |
| Critical success factors | Executive sponsorship #1 | Tooling/catalog/policies over sponsorship |
| Metrics | Process compliance ≠ business value | Measuring activity instead of outcomes |
| DAMA Wheel center | DG as enabling framework for all KAs | "Implemented first" or "most valuable" |
| Compliance role | Lineage/traceability for BCBS 239, GDPR | DQ scorecard instead of lineage |
| Stewardship types | Business, technical, coordinating stewards | Steward = owner |
